Reliable Weekly Turf Care

Professional Lawn Mowing & Precision Edging

Keep your lawn looking pristine without spending your weekends behind a mower. Our weekly lawn mowing service provides consistent, premium care that maximizes the green vigor of your Bermuda or Fescue turf.

At JD's Lawn & Landscaping, we treat your lawn like it's our own. We adjust our cutting heights based on seasonal turf conditions and grass type, ensuring we never scalp your lawn. Every standard service includes professional lawn mowing with sharp commercial blades, precision string trimming around fences and obstacles, hard edging along concrete driveways and walkways, and complete leaf-blowing cleanup of all concrete clippings.

What is Included in Our Mowing Maintenance

We operate on a regular weekly schedule from spring through fall, providing comprehensive lawn service:

  • ✔
    Sharp commercial blade mowing: We sharpen our mower blades daily to ensure a clean slice that prevents grass tip yellowing and disease.
  • ✔
    Vertical concrete mechanical edging: We cut clean vertical lines along driveways, patios, and sidewalks using steel-blade stick edgers (not string trimmers).
  • ✔
    Clipping leaf blower cleanup: We blow all grass clippings and debris off your porch, driveway, deck, and landscape beds before leaving.

Complete Property Care

A striped, neat lawn makes your entire property shine. For the ultimate curb appeal, many of our weekly mowing clients combine turf care with seasonal spade-cut borders and wood Mulch & Bed Edging, ornamental plant installation through Landscape Design, or leveling slope boundaries using dry-stack Retaining Walls.
