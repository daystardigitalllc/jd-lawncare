Healthy Soil & Crisp Borders

Sharp Shovel-Cut Borders & Premium Mulch Installation

Nothing defines a residential garden bed like a razor-sharp, hand-cut edge. Our mulch and edging services are designed to give your property an clean, neat border while protecting the health of your soil and plant root systems.

JD's Lawn & Landscaping provides professional double-shredded hardwood mulch and soil top-off services. We refuse to use cheap, thin mulch that washes away in the first rain. Instead, we use premium organic mulch (available in dark brown, matte black, or brick red) that suppresses weeds, retains vital soil moisture, and naturally decomposes to add nutrients back into your landscape beds.

The Importance of Shovel-Cut Edging

Many lawn care providers use mechanical string-trimmers or plastic borders to define flower beds. We believe in doing it the right way. Our crew cuts a deep, 3-to-4 inch vertical spade-border separating your grass turf from the garden bed. This:

  • ✔
    Prevents Grass Encroachment: It acts as a physical barrier that stops Bermuda and Fescue grass roots from spreading into your plant beds.
  • ✔
    Contains Mulch: It creates a trench that captures escaping mulch during heavy rains, keeping your driveway and turf clean.
  • ✔
    Elevates Visual Contrast: The shadow line created by a deep vertical edge makes the green grass and dark mulch contrast beautifully.

Maximized for Your Home's Health

Our process includes removing weeds, applying a pre-emergent weed barrier, styling root bases, and leveling topsoil. It's the perfect preparation before executing a custom Landscape Design or adding structural terracing like brick Retaining Walls.
