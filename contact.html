Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Find answers to basic questions regarding our schedules, licenses, and estimates.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, absolutely! We provide free, transparent, flat-rate on-site consultations and estimates for all our services in Clarksville, TN and surrounding areas. No pressure, no obligations.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, JD's Lawn & Landscaping is a fully licensed and insured business. We protect our workers, our clients, and your property on every project we execute.

How often do you mow lawns?

We offer weekly scheduled lawn care maintenance. Consistent scheduling ensures your grass stays healthy, neat, mowed to the proper height, and striped cleanly.

What areas do you serve?

We serve residential and commercial properties throughout Clarksville, TN and immediate surrounding areas (including Sango, Woodlawn, Oak Grove, KY, and Fort Campbell).

How soon can you start my project?

Depending on the service and current seasonal demand, we typically schedule consultations within 2-3 business days and begin installations within 1-2 weeks after estimate approval.
