Site Prep & Land Grading

Excavating & Site Grading Services in Clarksville, TN

Every hardscape, retaining wall, or drainage fix starts below the surface. We provide professional excavating and site grading throughout Clarksville, Sango, Oak Grove, Hopkinsville, Springfield, and Pleasant View — correcting poor drainage, clearing overgrown land, and preparing solid, compacted bases before a single stone or paver goes down.

Skipping proper excavation is the #1 reason DIY patios sink, driveways rut, and yards keep flooding after every storm. We use skid steers and mini excavators to cut to the correct depth, re-grade slopes away from your foundation, and haul off excess dirt and debris — so whatever gets built on top stays level for decades, not seasons.

Our Excavating & Grading Services

Depending on your property's needs, our excavation work includes:

  • ✔
    Drainage Correction & Regrading: Re-sloping yards and cutting swales to move water away from foundations, driveways, and low-lying beds that won't stop pooling.
  • ✔
    Land Clearing & Lot Prep: Removing brush, stumps, and overgrowth to prepare raw or neglected lots for new lawn, beds, or hardscape construction.
  • ✔
    Driveway & Base Excavation: Cutting and compacting proper sub-base depth for gravel, paver, and concrete driveways so they don't shift or wash out.
  • ✔
    Dirt & Debris Hauling: Full removal of excess spoil, rock, and construction debris — no piles left behind on your property.

The Foundation for Everything Else We Build

Excavation is the first step behind almost every project we run. Once the site is cut, graded, and compacted correctly, we can build a Retaining Wall that won't bulge, install a Paver Patio that won't sink, or finish it all off with fresh Landscape Design and Mulch & Bed Edging.
