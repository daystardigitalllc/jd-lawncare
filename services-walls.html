Erosion Control & Grading

Durable Stone & Block Retaining Wall Construction

Sloping yards can lead to severe soil erosion, poor lawn drainage, and unusable spaces. Our custom-built retaining walls solve structural grading challenges while adding value and terraced elegance to your property.

At JD's Lawn & Landscaping, we engineer retaining walls built for durability. A poorly constructed wall will bulge and collapse within a few seasons due to water pressure. We prevent this by laying reinforced aggregate gravel foundations, setting solid levels, and installing dedicated drainage pipes behind the block rows to relieve hydrostatic water pressure.

Our Retaining Wall Materials & Types

Depending on your yard's unique grading requirements and style preferences, we install:

  • ✔
    Segmental Block Walls: Heavy interlocking concrete blocks available in various colors and styles, perfect for structural strength and clean lines.
  • ✔
    Natural Dry-Stack Rock Borders: Utilizing flat fieldstone or flagstones to construct natural, organic terraced borders for custom plant beds.
  • ✔
    Terraced Garden Borders: Tiered multi-level walls built to organize slope sections into manageable flower gardens or lawn platforms.

Enhancing Your Outdoor Structure

Retaining walls function as the structural skeleton of a sloped yard. Once grade levels are secured, we can integrate custom stone steps leading up to your Paver Patio, lay decorative plant borders via Landscape Design, or finish borders with sharp Mulch & Bed Edging.
