Our History

Local roots. Family values.

Established in 2021, JD's Lawn & Landscaping began with a simple mission: to provide the homeowners and businesses of Clarksville, TN with premium, reliable outdoor services that make them proud of their properties.

Our team has over 10+ years of local lawn and landscaping experience. Over the years, we've grown from a single mower into a professional, full-service landscaping crew capable of executing custom landscape designs, laying sharp mulch borders, constructing stone retaining walls, and installing gorgeous paver patios.

Despite our growth, we remain a family-owned business at heart. We treat every yard as if it were our own, adhering strictly to our "good enough isn't how we work" philosophy. When you work with us, you are guaranteed clean borders, neat mowing lines, punctual arrivals, and clear, respectful communication.
