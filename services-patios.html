Premium Hardscapes

Custom Concrete Paver Patios & Flagstone Walkways

Take your indoor living comfort outside. We design and install custom hardscapes that turn blank yards into premium entertainment patios, relaxing flagstone walkways, and outdoor fire pit gathering areas.

Hardscape installations are major investments that require precise foundation engineering. At JD's Lawn & Landscaping, we refuse to cut corners. We excavate the site to proper depths, lay a heavy-duty geotextile fabric separator, build a thick crushed-gravel aggregate base compacted to standard specifications, level with washed bedding sand, and secure pavers with polymeric joint sand to prevent shifting or weed growth.

Our Hardscaping Installation Services

Our professional stone-masons construct custom outdoor features including:

  • ✔
    Concrete Paver Patios: Standard interlocking pavers, modern slab tiles, or traditional brick patterns in diverse shades, textures, and custom border bands.
  • ✔
    Flagstone Walkways: Charming natural stone pathways and walkways, laid irregular or cut-flat, to link garden spaces.
  • ✔
    Built-In Fire Pits: Circular or square block fire pits, matched to patio paver colors, perfect for cool Tennessee evenings.

Silo Integration & Custom Landscaping

A stunning hardscape looks best when integrated with clean green frames. We recommend pairing your new patio installation with surrounding flower garden beds via Landscape Design, crisp double-shredded Mulch & Bed Edging, or structural slope leveling utilizing brick Retaining Walls.
