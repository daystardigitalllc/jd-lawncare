How Much Does a Retaining Wall Cost in Clarksville, TN?

Home • Blog • Pricing & Planning
Pricing & Planning

If you're dealing with a sloped yard, soil erosion, or a drainage problem, a retaining wall is often the fix — but pricing questions usually come up before the design ones. Here's what actually determines what a retaining wall costs in Clarksville, TN and the surrounding area.

The Main Cost Factors

  • ✔
    Wall height and length. Cost scales with the total square footage of wall face. A short 20-foot garden border costs far less than a 4-foot structural wall running the length of a backyard.
  • ✔
    Material. Segmental interlocking block is typically the most budget-friendly option for structural strength. Natural dry-stack stone costs more but gives a custom, organic look.
  • ✔
    Drainage requirements. Any wall over roughly 2 feet needs a reinforced gravel base and a drainage pipe behind it to relieve hydrostatic pressure — skipping this is the #1 reason cheap walls fail within a few years.
  • ✔
    Site access and excavation. A wall on a steep slope or behind a fence with no equipment access takes longer and costs more than a wall built on flat, open ground.

Why We Don't Publish a Flat Per-Foot Price

Every yard in Clarksville is different — soil type, existing grade, and access all change the labor involved. Rather than quote a generic per-foot number that won't match your actual property, we do a free on-site evaluation and give you a flat-rate estimate before any work starts, so you know the full cost upfront with no surprises later.

What a Properly Built Wall Includes

A wall built the right way isn't just stacked block. It includes a compacted aggregate base below grade, a drainage pipe and gravel backfill behind the wall, and proper batter (a slight backward lean) so the structure resists soil pressure over time. This is the difference between a wall that lasts 30+ years and one that bulges or collapses within a few seasons.
